# Community 

A community is a place where members feel a sense of belonging and a desire to work together towards shared goals.

    Belonging without alignment => goodwill without cohesion
    Alignment without belonging => shared vision without empowerment or willingness

# Community engagement

Community engagement is the set of activities that facilitate the productive functioning of a team, group, or community

# What do community managers do?

Improve communication between group members, facilitate integration of members, work to counter-act power imbalances…

# Content and programming

Content and programming supports:

    - signposting of ways to engage
    - community members in getting to know each other
    - determination of shared interests and activities
    - sharing of information - within and beyond the community
    - the building of trust, reputation and social capital
    - reinforcement of community values and establishing community rituals (events, celebrations, expressing gratitude, etc)

> Content: Any written, audio or visual materials you make that are related to the functioning of your community. Content includes blogposts, newsarticles, reports, infographics, photos, videos, podcasts etc

> Programming: Activities that require sustained, facilitated interactions between members (e.g. events, projects, initiatives). Content is required to produce programming. Programming often involves the generation of new content.
-> Content synthesized, packaged and shared in a way intended to generate engagement

# Scaffolding

**Purposes**
- Lower barriers (info, technical, or sense of belonging) to participation 
- Support multi-modal participation (i.e. engagement in multiple modes of the community participation model (CPM))
- Can help prevent feelings of disengagement or exclusion that arise due to:
    - Lack of information, e.g. being unaware of ways to engage
    - Lack of tech support, e.g. not knowing how to use a tool or platform, lack of care for accessibility like alt-text
    - Reduced sense of belonging, e.g. feeling unable to share their specific needs or to express aspects of their personality
